Delete your data

Updated 9 November 2026

You can delete the data Nodely keeps about you at any time. Here's how, for each place we keep it.

Your game data (the Nodely app)

In the app, open Settings → Account and tap Delete account (or Delete my data if you never created an account). This permanently removes from our server:

  • your progress, scores, league and leaderboard entries, Daily results and duels;
  • your account (username), your recovery email, friends, friend requests and invite records;
  • the usage events tied to your player ID;
  • your Founder code redemption.

It happens straight away; backups roll over within 30 days. Purchases stay recorded by Google Play and RevenueCat, as their policies and the law require.

Can't open the app? Email braileanuolimpiu@gmail.com with your Nodely username (or the name shown in the game) and we'll delete it within 30 days.

To remove only your recovery email, open Settings → Account → Recovery email and tap Remove.

Launch news emails

If you signed up on our website to hear about the launch, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email: it deletes your address straight away. You can also email us and we'll remove it.

Instagram and Facebook (Founder codes)

If you asked for a Founder code on Instagram, we keep only your Instagram username, the Instagram-scoped ID Meta gives us for your messages, and the code we sent you, so each follower gets exactly one code. We don't keep the content of your messages, and we don't use Facebook Login.

To have this removed, send DELETE MY DATA as a direct message to our Instagram account, or email braileanuolimpiu@gmail.com with your Instagram username. We delete it within 30 days. A code you already redeemed in the game stays linked to your game account until you delete that too (see above).

Questions

Write to braileanuolimpiu@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
