Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Nodely is a puzzle game made by an independent developer, Olimpiu Braileanu, based in the Republic of Moldova ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Nodely app and its game server collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Write to braileanuolimpiu@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without giving us your name, email or phone number.
  • We store your game progress so leaderboards, leagues, friends and duels work and so you can keep your progress on a new phone.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and purchases go through Google Play and RevenueCat. These services process some device data under their own policies.
  • We never sell your data.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Game data, stored on our server

  • A random player ID that the app creates the first time you open it. It's not your phone's hardware ID, and it doesn't contain any personal details.
  • Your player name and avatar. The app generates a random name, which you can change. These are visible to other players on leaderboards and in Friends.
  • Game progress: levels reached, coins, scores, records, quests, league and Season progress, Blitz and Endless scores, and themes and items you own or wear.
  • Puzzle solutions you submit. The server checks that a result was really solved before it counts for scores and rewards.
  • Friends and duels: who you are friends with, friend requests, and duel times and results.
  • An optional account: if you create one, a username and a password. We store the password only as a secure one-way hash, so nobody can read it, and we keep a backup of your progress so you can sign in on another device.

Technical and security data

  • IP address: processed by our server to limit abuse, for example too many attempts. It's used for security only and is not used to build a profile of you.
  • Google Play Integrity: on Android the app may ask Google whether it is a genuine copy running on a genuine device. We receive only Google's verdict, which we use to stop cheating.
  • Rewarded-ad confirmations: when you watch an ad for a reward, Google tells our server that the ad was completed, so the reward can't be faked.

Stored only on your phone

  • Settings (sound, language, dark mode, colour-blind letters), your current level and reminder times. Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone; we don't send push notifications through a server.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, precise location or payment card details.

How we use information

  • To run the game: save progress, show leaderboards, leagues, friends, duels and Blitz rankings, and restore your progress on a new device.
  • To keep it fair: verify solutions and rewards, and block cheating and abuse.
  • To show ads and deliver purchases.
  • To fix problems and improve the game.

Our legal bases, where the GDPR applies, are performing our agreement with you (running the game), our legitimate interests (security, fair play, improving the game) and your consent (personalised ads).

What other players can see

Your player name, avatar, level, league, scores and duel results can be seen by other players. Don't use your real name or anything private as your player name.

How long we keep it

We keep game data while you play. If you delete your data, it is removed from our server straight away; backups roll over within 30 days. Players who haven't played for a long time may have their data removed.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Account and tap Delete account, or Delete my data if you never created an account. This permanently removes your progress, scores, friends and account from our server. You can also email us to ask for deletion. Purchases stay recorded by Google Play and RevenueCat as their policies and the law require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Email us and we'll help within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Nodely is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and access to the server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Nodely Poster (our social-media tool)

We use a small internal tool, Nodely Poster, to publish our own promotional videos to our own Nodely accounts on TikTok and Instagram. It only accesses the accounts we connect to it, and only to read basic account information and publish our videos. Its access tokens are stored encrypted, and it doesn't collect information about anyone else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
