Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Nodely is a puzzle game made by an independent developer, Olimpiu Braileanu, based in the Republic of Moldova ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Nodely app and its game server collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Write to braileanuolimpiu@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without giving us your name, email or phone number. An email is only needed if you choose to add one for password recovery or to be told when Nodely launches.
  • We store your game progress, coins and items on our server so leaderboards, leagues, friends and duels work, so nobody can cheat, and so you can keep your progress on a new phone.
  • If you write to us from Help & support in the app, we keep your message and some technical details so we can help you.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and purchases go through Google Play and RevenueCat. These services process some device data under their own policies.
  • We never sell your data.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time, or follow the steps on our Delete your data page.

What we collect

Game data, stored on our server

  • A random player ID that the app creates the first time you open it. It's not your phone's hardware ID, and it doesn't contain any personal details. We also give each player a separate random ID that we share with RevenueCat and Google AdMob (see below); it's not your username or your email.
  • Your player name and avatar. The app generates a random name, which you can change. These are visible to other players on leaderboards, in leagues, in Friends and in duels.
  • Game progress: levels reached, scores, records, quests, league and Season progress, Blitz and Endless scores, and the themes and items you wear.
  • Coins and items: your coin balance and a record of the coins you earn and spend, the items you own (themes, styles, avatars and chest kits), your chest keys and the Season-pass rewards you've claimed. We keep these on our server so they can't be faked and so they follow your account to a new phone.
  • Puzzle solutions you submit. The server checks that a result was really solved before it counts for scores and rewards.
  • Friends and duels: who you are friends with, friend requests, and duel times and results.
  • Your account: needed after the first 10 levels: a username, a password and your birth year (to keep ads right for your age). We store the password only as a secure one-way hash, so nobody can read it, and we keep a backup of your progress so you can sign in on another device.

Optional email

  • Recovery email: if you are 13 or older and add one to your account (Settings → Account → Recovery email), we use it only to send you a code when you forget your password. Players under 13 are never asked for an email. It is never shown to other players, and you can remove it at any time.
  • Launch news: if you sign up on our website, we keep your email, the language of your browser and the date, and send you news about the launch. We first send you one confirmation email, and your address is used only after you click the link in it; addresses not confirmed within 7 days are deleted automatically. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, which deletes your address.

Help & support messages

If you write to us from Help & support in the app, we keep:

  • Your message: the topic you choose and the text you write (up to 2,000 characters), and our reply.
  • Details the app attaches, so we can check a problem without asking back and forth: the app version, platform (Android or iOS), language, your level, your coin balance, whether you have an account, whether you bought Remove Ads, whether your purchases are linked to your player ID, and the order IDs of up to 20 of your Google Play coin purchases.
  • What our server adds: its records of your Season-pass purchases and of any coin purchases that were refunded.

Messages are stored on our server. Our email delivery provider may send us an email copy of your message (the topic, the text and your player number) so we notice it quickly. We answer inside the app, in Help & support. Please don't put private information such as passwords or card numbers in a message.

Technical and security data

  • IP address: processed by our server to limit abuse, for example too many attempts. It's used for security only and is not used to build a profile of you.
  • Google Play Integrity: on Android the app may ask Google whether it is a genuine copy running on a genuine device. We receive only Google's verdict, which we use to stop cheating.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, it sends a technical report (the error, the app version, the phone model and operating system) to Sentry, so we can fix the bug. Crash reports contain no name, email, IP address or screenshots.
  • Usage events (our own analytics): the app tells our server about a few gameplay milestones, such as finishing the tutorial, reaching level 10, coming back the next day, or that an ad was shown or a purchase made. They are tied to your random player ID only, contain no personal details, and help us see where the game is too hard or confusing.
  • Rewarded-ad confirmations: when you watch an ad for a reward, Google AdMob tells our server directly that your random player ID finished an ad and what the reward was for (for example extra coins or a hint), so the reward can't be faked.

Stored only on your phone

  • Settings (sound, language, dark mode, colour-blind letters), your current level and reminder times. Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone; we don't send push notifications through a server.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, precise location or payment card details.

Invites and Founder codes

Invite links. If you join from a friend's invite link, Google Play passes the invite code to the app when it's first installed (through the Google Play Install Referrer). When you create an account we record who invited whom, make you friends, and count it toward the inviter's reward.

Founder codes. If you ask for a Founder code by direct message on Instagram, we check with Instagram whether you follow our account and keep your Instagram username, the Instagram-scoped ID Meta gives us for your messages, and the code we sent you, so each follower gets one code. We don't keep the content of your messages. When you redeem the code in the app, our server records which game account redeemed it.

See Delete your data to remove any of this.

How we use information

  • To run the game: save progress, show leaderboards, leagues, friends, duels and Blitz rankings, and restore your progress on a new device.
  • To keep it fair: verify solutions and rewards, and block cheating and abuse.
  • To show ads and deliver purchases.
  • To answer your support messages and sort out problems with purchases.
  • To fix problems and improve the game.

Data sent abroad

Some of the services above (Google, RevenueCat, Sentry) process data outside the Republic of Moldova and the EU, mainly in the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or similar safeguards.

Our legal bases, where the GDPR applies, are performing our agreement with you (running the game), our legitimate interests (security, fair play, improving the game) and your consent (personalised ads, launch news). In the Republic of Moldova we process personal data under Law no. 195/2024 on personal data protection, which follows the same principles, and you can complain to the National Center for Personal Data Protection (datepersonale.md).

What other players can see

Your username or player name, avatar, level, league, scores and duel results can be seen by other players on leaderboards, in leagues, in Friends and in duels. Don't use your real name or anything private as your player name. Names are checked automatically, and players can report a name or block a player inside the app. If a reported name breaks our rules, we may replace it with a generated name. Your birth year, recovery email and support messages are never shown to other players.

How long we keep it

  • Game data, coins, items and account: while you play. If you delete your data, it is removed from our server straight away; backups roll over within 30 days. Players who haven't played for two years may have their data removed. Guest players (no account) who never solved a puzzle and never earned or bought anything are deleted automatically 30 days after they first opened the app.
  • Support messages: deleted with your account. Once a conversation is closed, it is deleted after 30 days for players under 13 and after 12 months for everyone else.
  • Recovery email: until you remove it or delete your account. Reset codes expire after 30 minutes.
  • Launch news email: until you unsubscribe, and no longer than one year after the launch.
  • Usage events: up to 13 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Security logs (IP addresses): up to 30 days.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Account and tap Delete account, or Delete my data if you never created an account. This permanently removes your progress, scores, friends and account from our server. This also deletes your coins, items and support messages. You can also email us to ask for deletion. Full instructions, including Instagram data for Founder codes: Delete your data. Purchases stay recorded by Google Play and RevenueCat as their policies and the law require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Email us and we'll help within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Nodely is made for everyone, children included. The first 10 levels need no account; after that, playing needs a free account, and creating it asks how old you are. We keep only the birth year with your account (never a birthday), so the right settings follow you to any phone; it is never shown to other players. It only decides how the app behaves:

  • Under 13: only child-safe ads (rated for all ages, never personalized, no tracking for advertising; ad requests are tagged as child-directed in AdMob), and the app never asks for an email address. We keep only what's needed to run the game.
  • 13 to 15: ads are never personalized.
  • Everyone: ads are limited to family-friendly content, player names are checked automatically and inappropriate names are blocked, and there is no chat between players.

An account needs only a username and password, no real name. Parents or guardians can ask us at any time to see or delete a child’s data: write to braileanuolimpiu@gmail.com, or delete it straight from the app in Settings → Account.

How long we keep children's data

For players under 13 we keep only what the game needs to work:

  • The random player IDs, username, password hash and birth year: while the account exists.
  • Game progress, coins, items, friends and duel results: while the account exists, so progress isn't lost and can move to a new phone.
  • Usage events: up to 13 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days. Security logs (IP addresses): up to 30 days.
  • Support messages: deleted 30 days after the conversation is closed.

Everything is deleted when the account is deleted (from the app, at a parent's request, or after two years without playing); backups roll over within 30 days. Guest players who never played are deleted after 30 days. We never use children's data to build advertising profiles, and we don't keep it longer than needed for the purposes above.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and access to the server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Nodely Poster (our social-media tool)

We use a small internal tool, Nodely Poster, to publish our own promotional videos to our own Nodely accounts on TikTok and Instagram. It only accesses the accounts we connect to it, and only to read basic account information and publish our videos. Its access tokens are stored encrypted, and it doesn't collect information about anyone else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
