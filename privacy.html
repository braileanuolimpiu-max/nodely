Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Nodely is a puzzle game made by an independent developer, Olimpiu Braileanu, based in the Republic of Moldova ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Nodely app and its game server collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions? Write to braileanuolimpiu@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without giving us your name, email or phone number. An email is only needed if you choose to add one for password recovery or to be told when Nodely launches.
  • We store your game progress so leaderboards, leagues, friends and duels work and so you can keep your progress on a new phone.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and purchases go through Google Play and RevenueCat. These services process some device data under their own policies.
  • We never sell your data.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

Game data, stored on our server

  • A random player ID that the app creates the first time you open it. It's not your phone's hardware ID, and it doesn't contain any personal details.
  • Your player name and avatar. The app generates a random name, which you can change. These are visible to other players on leaderboards and in Friends.
  • Game progress: levels reached, coins, scores, records, quests, league and Season progress, Blitz and Endless scores, and themes and items you own or wear.
  • Puzzle solutions you submit. The server checks that a result was really solved before it counts for scores and rewards.
  • Friends and duels: who you are friends with, friend requests, and duel times and results.
  • Your account: needed after the first 10 levels: a username, a password and your birth year (to keep ads right for your age). We store the password only as a secure one-way hash, so nobody can read it, and we keep a backup of your progress so you can sign in on another device.

Optional email

  • Recovery email: if you add one to your account (Settings → Account → Recovery email), we use it only to send you a code when you forget your password. It is never shown to other players, and you can remove it at any time.
  • Launch news: if you sign up on our website, we keep your email, the language of your browser and the date, and send you news about the launch. We first send you one confirmation email, and your address is used only after you click the link in it; addresses not confirmed within 7 days are deleted automatically. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, which deletes your address.

Technical and security data

  • IP address: processed by our server to limit abuse, for example too many attempts. It's used for security only and is not used to build a profile of you.
  • Google Play Integrity: on Android the app may ask Google whether it is a genuine copy running on a genuine device. We receive only Google's verdict, which we use to stop cheating.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, it sends a technical report (the error, the app version, the phone model and operating system) to Sentry, so we can fix the bug. Crash reports contain no name, email, IP address or screenshots.
  • Usage events: the app tells our server about a few milestones, such as finishing the tutorial, reaching level 10, coming back the next day, or that an ad was shown or a purchase made. They are tied to your random player ID only and help us see where the game is too hard or confusing.
  • Rewarded-ad confirmations: when you watch an ad for a reward, Google tells our server that the ad was completed, so the reward can't be faked.

Stored only on your phone

  • Settings (sound, language, dark mode, colour-blind letters), your current level and reminder times. Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone; we don't send push notifications through a server.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, precise location or payment card details.

Invites and Founder codes

Invite links. If you join from a friend's invite link, Google Play passes the invite code to the app when it's first installed. When you create an account we record who invited whom, make you friends, and count it toward the inviter's reward.

Founder codes. If you ask for a Founder code by direct message on Instagram, we check with Instagram whether you follow our account and keep your Instagram username, the Instagram-scoped ID Meta gives us for your messages, and the code we sent you, so each follower gets one code. We don't keep the content of your messages. When you redeem the code in the app, our server records which game account redeemed it.

See Delete your data to remove any of this.

How we use information

  • To run the game: save progress, show leaderboards, leagues, friends, duels and Blitz rankings, and restore your progress on a new device.
  • To keep it fair: verify solutions and rewards, and block cheating and abuse.
  • To show ads and deliver purchases.
  • To fix problems and improve the game.

Data sent abroad

Some of the services above (Google, RevenueCat, Sentry) process data outside the Republic of Moldova and the EU, mainly in the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or similar safeguards.

Our legal bases, where the GDPR applies, are performing our agreement with you (running the game), our legitimate interests (security, fair play, improving the game) and your consent (personalised ads, launch news). In the Republic of Moldova we process personal data under Law no. 195/2024 on personal data protection, which follows the same principles, and you can complain to the National Center for Personal Data Protection (datepersonale.md).

What other players can see

Your player name, avatar, level, league, scores and duel results can be seen by other players. Don't use your real name or anything private as your player name.

How long we keep it

  • Game data and account: while you play. If you delete your data, it is removed from our server straight away; backups roll over within 30 days. Players who haven't played for two years may have their data removed.
  • Recovery email: until you remove it or delete your account. Reset codes expire after 30 minutes.
  • Launch news email: until you unsubscribe, and no longer than one year after the launch.
  • Usage events: up to 13 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Security logs (IP addresses): up to 30 days.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Account and tap Delete account, or Delete my data if you never created an account. This permanently removes your progress, scores, friends and account from our server. You can also email us to ask for deletion. Full instructions, including Instagram data for Founder codes: Delete your data. Purchases stay recorded by Google Play and RevenueCat as their policies and the law require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Email us and we'll help within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Nodely is made for everyone, children included. The first 10 levels need no account; after that, playing needs a free account, and creating it asks how old you are. We keep only the birth year with your account (never a birthday), so the right settings follow you to any phone; it is never shown to other players. It only decides how the app behaves:

  • Under 13: only child-safe ads (rated for all ages, never personalized, no tracking for advertising), and the app never asks for an email address.
  • 13 to 15: ads are never personalized.
  • Everyone: ads are limited to family-friendly content, player names are checked automatically and inappropriate names are blocked, and there is no chat between players.

An account needs only a username and password, no real name. Parents or guardians can ask us at any time to see or delete a child’s data: write to braileanuolimpiu@gmail.com, or delete it straight from the app in Settings → Account.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and access to the server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Nodely Poster (our social-media tool)

We use a small internal tool, Nodely Poster, to publish our own promotional videos to our own Nodely accounts on TikTok and Instagram. It only accesses the accounts we connect to it, and only to read basic account information and publish our videos. Its access tokens are stored encrypted, and it doesn't collect information about anyone else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
